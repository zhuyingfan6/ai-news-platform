# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-24

U.S. supply chains face mounting cost and policy pressures this week, with diesel prices surging 31% year-over-year and new Canada tariffs taking effect, while infrastructure investments and leadership changes signal shifts in freight strategy.

**Policy & Trade**

- The U.S. imposed new 50% tariffs on Canadian machinery, electrical equipment, and other goods, though energy and critical minerals remain exempt, reshaping cross-border trade flows. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/trump-canada-tariffs-products-affected)

**Freight & Logistics**

- The Port of New Orleans received federal approval for a $1.8 billion container terminal in Violet, Louisiana, designed to accommodate larger cargo ships and boost regional capacity. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/new-orleans-port-louisiana-container-terminal-federal-permit)

- U.S. diesel prices climbed 31% year-over-year to $4.96 per gallon in July, adding roughly $177 to a typical 150-gallon fill-up for trucking fleets. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/diesel-prices-july-2026-year-over-year-trucking-fuel-costs)
