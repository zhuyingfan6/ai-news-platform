# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-24

U.S. supply chains face rising cost pressures from surging diesel prices and new Canada tariffs, while infrastructure investments and AI adoption reshape logistics strategies.

**Trade & Tariffs**

- The U.S. and Canada are locked in a new tariff dispute, with a 50% U.S. levy covering machinery and electrical equipment, while energy and critical minerals remain exempt. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/trump-canada-tariffs-products-affected)

**Freight & Fuel Costs**

- U.S. diesel prices have climbed 31% year over year to $4.96 per gallon, adding roughly $177 to a typical 150-gallon fill-up for trucking fleets. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/diesel-prices-july-2026-year-over-year-trucking-fuel-costs)

**Infrastructure & Ports**

- The Port of New Orleans has secured federal approval for a $1.8 billion container terminal in Violet, Louisiana, designed to accommodate larger cargo ships. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/new-orleans-port-louisiana-container-terminal-federal-permit)
