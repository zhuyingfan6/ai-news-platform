# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-05

Supply chain decision-making is being reshaped by new data integrations in fleet dispatch, while significant market shifts are underway in last-mile delivery and European logistics real estate. At the same time, a new Gartner survey reveals a major gap between AI investment and executive confidence in its returns.

**Fleet Management & Technology**
- BeyondTrucks and VLC are integrating driver and vehicle compliance data directly into fleet dispatch and load-assignment decisions. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/beyondtrucks-vlc-driver-safety-compliance-dispatch)
