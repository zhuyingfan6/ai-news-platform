# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-09-09

Supply chain operations are bracing for peak season cost pressures while expanding automation, infrastructure, and precision technology across North America and beyond. Key developments include new warehouse deployments in Canada, cross-border truck charging corridors, and strategic acquisitions to enhance logistics visibility.

**Warehouse & Fulfillment Operations**
- Kardex is bringing its AutoStore warehouse automation business to Canada, leveraging its North American staff and local partner network to expand its footprint. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/kardex-expands-autostore-warehouse-business-canada)

- Warehouses can prevent costly trailer misloads and reduce dock delays by sequencing picking, staging, and trailer loading around each route’s specific stops, improving delivery speed and accuracy. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/plan-trailer-loading-before-warehouse-picking)

**Transportation & Infrastructure**
- Five governments are collaborating on a truck charging and hydrogen fueling corridor along the West Coast, stretching from British Columbia to Baja California, to support zero-emission freight. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/west-coast-electric-truck-charging-corridor)

**Peak Season Strategy**
- As peak season approaches, shippers still have time to cut costs by testing new carriers, reviewing packaging, and catching delivery problems early, according to DCL’s Brian Tu. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/how-shippers-can-cut-peak-season-costs)

**Technology & Data**
- Trimble has acquired Terasat Japan, expanding its high-precision positioning service through a network of over 1,300 reference stations to enhance supply chain tracking and navigation. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/trimble-acquires-terasat-japan-positioning-network)
