# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-31

Supply chain investments and technology shifts dominated the day's news, with major new infrastructure projects announced in North America and new data-driven tools emerging for insurance and retail inventory management.

- Walmart plans a $1.3 billion automated fulfillment center in Carnesville, Georgia, expected to create 1,000 jobs and enable faster shipping. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/walmart-georgia-automated-fulfillment-center)
