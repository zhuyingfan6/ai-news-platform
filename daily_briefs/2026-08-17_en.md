# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-17

Supply chain networks are shifting as companies respond to tariff pressures, invest in advanced manufacturing, and adopt digital tools to improve resilience. Key moves include Ford relocating Lincoln production to the U.S., Amazon expanding its Northeast footprint, and Moog opening a high-tech defense plant.

**Manufacturing & Production Shifts**

- Ford will move production of U.S.-market Lincoln vehicles from China to American plants starting in 2030, driven by rising tariffs on imported goods. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ford-lincoln-production-china-us-2030)

- Moog opened a $150 million manufacturing facility in Western New York, using automation and robotics to produce parts for military aircraft, including the F-35. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/moog-150-million-new-york-manufacturing-plant)

**Warehousing & Fulfillment Expansion**

- Amazon is evaluating a nearly 1.1 million-square-foot fulfillment center in eastern Connecticut, which would create about 500 jobs and include space for over 420 trailers. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/amazon-1-million-square-foot-connecticut-warehouse)

**Digital Supply Chain Innovation**

- Target is leveraging its Proxima digital twin technology to simulate inventory changes and identify supply chain disruptions before they impact store operations. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/target-expands-proxima-supply-chain-digital-twin)
