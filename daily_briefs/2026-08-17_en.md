# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-17

U.S. manufacturers are reshoring production and investing heavily in domestic capacity, driven by tariff pressures and defense demand. Key moves include Ford shifting Lincoln output from China and Moog opening a major new plant in New York.

**Manufacturing & Reshoring**

- Ford will move production of U.S.-market Lincoln vehicles from China to American plants starting in 2030, citing rising tariff costs on imports. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ford-lincoln-production-china-us-2030)

- Moog has opened a $150 million manufacturing facility in Western New York, using automation and robotics to produce parts for military aircraft, including the F-35. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/moog-150-million-new-york-manufacturing-plant)
