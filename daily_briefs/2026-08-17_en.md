# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-17

U.S. automaker Ford is shifting Lincoln production from China to domestic plants by 2030 amid rising tariffs, while aerospace supplier Moog opens a major new automated factory in New York for military aircraft parts.

**Manufacturing & Production**

- Ford will move production of U.S.-market Lincoln vehicles from China to American plants beginning in 2030, as tariffs raise import costs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ford-lincoln-production-china-us-2030)

- Moog opened a $150 million New York factory that will use automation and robotics to make parts for military aircraft, including the F-35. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/moog-150-million-new-york-manufacturing-plant)
