# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-17

Supply chain networks are shifting as companies respond to tariff pressures, invest in advanced manufacturing, and adopt digital tools to improve resilience. Key moves include reshoring automotive production, expanding warehouse footprints, and leveraging AI-driven simulations for proactive planning.

**Manufacturing & Reshoring**
- Ford will move production of U.S.-market Lincoln vehicles from China to American plants starting in 2030, citing rising import tariffs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ford-lincoln-production-china-us-2030)
- Moog opened a $150 million factory in Western New York, using automation and robotics to produce parts for military aircraft, including the F-35. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/moog-150-million-new-york-manufacturing-plant)

**Warehousing & Logistics**
- Amazon is evaluating a nearly 1.1 million-square-foot fulfillment center in eastern Connecticut, which would create about 500 jobs and include space for over 420 trailers. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/amazon-1-million-square-foot-connecticut-warehouse)

**Digital Supply Chain & Technology**
- Target is expanding use of its Proxima digital twin to simulate inventory changes and detect supply chain disruptions before they impact store operations. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/target-expands-proxima-supply-chain-digital-twin)
