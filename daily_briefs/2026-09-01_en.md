# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-09-01

Supply chain leaders are navigating a wave of major investments and strategic shifts, from multi-million-dollar robotics and software acquisitions to billion-dollar manufacturing expansions, while also preparing for key 2026 priorities like automation and resilience.

- Locus Robotics has raised $41.6 million in Series G funding from existing investors, with the round remaining open to additional capital to support its warehouse automation growth. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/locus-robotics-41-million-series-g-funding)
