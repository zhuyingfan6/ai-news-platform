# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-11-19

NYC delivery regulations and trucker restroom access advanced in policy news, while UPS moved to expand healthcare logistics in Australia and New Zealand, AdventHealth topped Gartner's healthcare supply chain rankings, and a survey showed most operations leaders see AI's revenue potential but few are redesigning processes around it.

## Regulation & Policy

- A New York City bill would require licenses and direct hiring at some last-mile facilities, potentially adding up to $5 per order for Amazon and putting its contracts with over 40 local delivery firms at risk [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/nyc-amazon-delivery-contractor-bill)

- A Senate panel advanced a bipartisan bill that would give truck drivers access to existing restrooms at covered businesses during pickups and deliveries [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/senate-panel-advances-trucker-bathroom-access-bill)

## Healthcare Supply Chains

- AdventHealth leads Gartner's 2026 healthcare supply chain rankings for a third consecutive year, with Kaiser Permanente debuting at No. 6 [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/adventhealth-2026-healthcare-supply-chain-top-25)

- UPS agreed to acquire Smartways Logistics, a courier delivering medical devices and other urgent healthcare shipments across Australia and New Zealand [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ups-acquires-smartways-healthcare-logistics)

## Technology & AI

- An Accenture survey found 72% of operations leaders see more potential for AI to grow revenue than to cut costs, while only 15% are redesigning processes around it [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/survey-few-executives-are-building-their-work-processes-around-ai)
