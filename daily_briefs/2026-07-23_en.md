# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-07-23

**Supply chain activity stalled in contraction during June, while trucking costs hit a record high and major rail and aviation investments signaled long-term shifts in logistics infrastructure.**

### Mergers & Rail
- Union Pacific and CN reached a deal tied to the proposed $85B Norfolk Southern merger, with CN dropping opposition in exchange for expanded Midwest access. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/cn-union-pacific-norfolk-southern-merger-access-deal)

### Logistics & Costs
- A new Warehouse on Wheels index shows U.S. supply chain activity remained in contraction during June, barely changing from the previous month. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/supply-chain-activity-remains-in-contraction)
