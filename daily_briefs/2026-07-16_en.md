# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-07-16

TSMC and Maersk announce major U.S. investments in chip and fulfillment infrastructure, while a new report highlights five emerging countries for supply chain expansion and Boston Dynamics pitches its robot dog for last-mile delivery.

**Manufacturing & Investment**
- TSMC will invest another $100 billion in Arizona, bringing its total U.S. commitment to $265 billion and adding four new chip fabs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/tsmc-100-billion-arizona-chip-manufacturing-expansion)

- Maersk is opening a $100 million fulfillment center in Hopedale, Massachusetts, creating about 1,000 jobs and expanding its Northeast logistics network. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/maersk-hopedale-massachusetts-fulfillment-center)

**Global Expansion & Strategy**
- A new Verisk Maplecroft report identifies Thailand, the Philippines, Argentina, Uruguay and Chile as five countries ripe for future supply chain expansion. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/new-report-five-countries-supply-chain-expansion)

**Last-Mile & Robotics**
- Boston Dynamics is pitching its Spot robot dog as a last-mile delivery assistant and is seeking logistics partners for pilot programs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/boston-dynamics-spot-robot-dog-last-mile-delivery)
