# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-11-05

John Deere is shifting production to Brazil amid U.S. plant closures, while Walmart scales drone delivery and Freeport invests in domestic copper mining; energy constraints and warehouse automation also shape the supply chain landscape.

## Manufacturing & Production Shifts

- John Deere plans to close two Iowa manufacturing plants and move sugar cane harvester production to Brazil, with work also redistributed across other U.S. factories [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/john-deere-iowa-plant-closures-production-moves-brazil)

- Freeport approved a $4.5 billion expansion of its Bagdad copper mine in Arizona to boost U.S. copper and molybdenum output, with initial production targeted for mid-2030 [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/freeport-bagdad-arizona-copper-mine-expansion)

## Logistics & Delivery

- Walmart plans 300 new Zipline drone delivery sites and has raised $1.5 billion to expand U.S. manufacturing, hiring, and service [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/walmart-zipline-300-drone-delivery-sites-funding)

## Warehouse Automation & Packaging

- Packsize and Swisslog are partnering to integrate on-demand box-making into automated warehouse systems, enabling workers to pick directly into shipping cartons [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/packsize-swisslog-warehouse-packaging-partnership)

## Energy & Infrastructure Risks
