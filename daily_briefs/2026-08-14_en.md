# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-14

Supply chain stakeholders are closely watching a major proposed rail merger, while Amazon continues to expand its logistics footprint through new automation agreements and physical pickup infrastructure. These developments signal shifts in both freight competition and last-mile delivery strategies.

- Seven state attorneys general are urging the Surface Transportation Board to reject the proposed $85 billion Union Pacific-NS merger, arguing it would reduce competition and raise shipping costs.
