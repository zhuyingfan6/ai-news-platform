# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-10-27

UPS is set to raise daily shipping rates by an average of 5.9% on Dec. 21, just days before Christmas, while new reports highlight labor compliance gaps in food supply chains, a $100 million FTC settlement for Shein, and record online grocery penetration in the U.S.

- UPS will raise daily shipping rates an average of 5.9% on Dec. 21, alongside changes to weight minimums, shipping zones, and fees [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ups-shipping-rate-increase-december-2026)

- Deposco found parcel rates fell 3.3% in Q3, but heavier packages and faster services limited actual shipping cost savings to just 0.6% [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/parcel-rates-fall-shippers-see-little-savings-deposco)
