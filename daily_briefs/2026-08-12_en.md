# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-12

Supply chain leaders are taking decisive action to shore up resilience, from GM’s $4.5 billion supplier financing fund to Walmart’s massive new warehouse plans, while the aerospace sector struggles with persistent bottlenecks and Uber Freight probes a major data breach.

**Corporate Strategy & Investment**

- General Motors has set aside $4.5 billion in a financing program to help suppliers buy and hold critical parts, protecting its supply chain against future disruptions. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/gm-4-billion-critical-parts-supply-fund)

- Walmart is eyeing a 1.5 million-square-foot distribution center in New York, featuring 1,050 truck spaces and creating roughly 1,500 jobs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/walmart-new-york-distribution-center-wallkill)
