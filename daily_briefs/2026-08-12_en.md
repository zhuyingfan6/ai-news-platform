# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-12

Supply chain resilience is taking center stage as major players invest in buffer capacity and face persistent disruptions, while cybersecurity threats and infrastructure expansion reshape the logistics landscape.

**Automotive & Manufacturing Resilience**
- General Motors has established a $4.5 billion financing program to help suppliers purchase and hold critical parts, aiming to shield its production from future supply chain disruptions. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/gm-4-billion-critical-parts-supply-fund)

- Despite booming aerospace demand, aircraft deliveries continue to lag due to persistent labor shortages, parts scarcity, and supplier bottlenecks, according to EY’s Raman Ram. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/aerospace-supply-chain-bottlenecks-aircraft-deliveries)

**Logistics & Infrastructure Expansion**
- Walmart is exploring a massive 1.5 million-square-foot distribution center in New York, which would feature 1,050 truck spaces and create approximately 1,500 jobs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/walmart-new-york-distribution-center-wallkill)

**Cybersecurity Threats in Freight**
- Uber Freight is investigating a potential data breach after hackers claimed to have stolen nearly 1 million files, although the company reports that business operations have not been disrupted. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/uber-freight-hackers-nearly-1-million-files)
