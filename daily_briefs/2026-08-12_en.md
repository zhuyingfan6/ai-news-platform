# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-08-12

Supply chain leaders are responding to a volatile landscape with major financial commitments, infrastructure expansions, and heightened security measures, even as persistent bottlenecks continue to challenge key sectors like aerospace.

**Corporate Strategy & Resilience**
- General Motors has established a $4.5 billion financing program to help its suppliers purchase and hold critical parts, aiming to shield its production from future supply chain disruptions. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/gm-4-billion-critical-parts-supply-fund)

- Walmart is exploring the development of a massive 1.5 million-square-foot distribution center in New York, which would feature 1,050 truck spaces and create approximately 1,500 jobs. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/walmart-new-york-distribution-center-wallkill)

**Sector-Specific Challenges**
- Despite booming aerospace demand, aircraft deliveries continue to lag due to persistent bottlenecks in labor, parts availability, and supplier capacity, according to EY’s Raman Ram. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/aerospace-supply-chain-bottlenecks-aircraft-deliveries)

**Cybersecurity Threats**
- Uber Freight is investigating a potential data breach after hackers claimed to have stolen nearly 1 million files, though the company asserts that its business operations were not disrupted. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/uber-freight-hackers-nearly-1-million-files)
