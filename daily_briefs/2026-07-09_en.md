# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-07-09

Global supply chains face heightened disruption risks as a new report warns that one-third of the world's busiest ports are vulnerable, while U.S. lawmakers push to restore a key manufacturing support program.

**Port & Infrastructure Risk**
- A Verisk Maplecroft report finds that about one-third of the world's busiest ports and airports face elevated disruption risks from conflict, weather, and other factors. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/report-one-third-of-worlds-busiest-ports-face-elevated-disruption-risk)

**Manufacturing Policy**
- A bipartisan group of U.S. senators has introduced a bill to restore funding for the Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP), a program that supports small and mid-sized manufacturers. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/senate-bill-restore-mep-manufacturing-funding)
