# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-07-09

Global supply chains face heightened disruption risks as a new report warns that one-third of the world's busiest ports are vulnerable to conflict and weather, while U.S. lawmakers push to restore funding for a key manufacturing support program.

- **Port & Infrastructure Risk:** A new Verisk Maplecroft report reveals that approximately one-third of the world's busiest ports and airports face elevated disruption risks from conflict, extreme weather, and other threats. - **Manufacturing Policy:** A bipartisan group of U.S. senators has introduced a bill to force the restoration of funding for the Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP), a program that provides critical support to small and mid-sized manufacturers.
