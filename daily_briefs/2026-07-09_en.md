# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-07-09

Global supply chains face mounting pressure as a new report warns that one-third of the world's busiest ports and airports are at high disruption risk, while U.S. lawmakers push to restore funding for a key manufacturing support program.

**Port & Infrastructure Risk**
- A Verisk Maplecroft report finds that roughly one-third of the world's busiest ports and airports face elevated disruption risks from conflict, extreme weather, and other threats. [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/report-one-third-of-worlds-busiest-ports-face-elevated-disruption-risk)

**Manufacturing Policy**
- A bipartisan group of U.S. senators has introduced a bill to force the restoration of funding for the Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP), a program that supports small and mid-sized manufacturers.
