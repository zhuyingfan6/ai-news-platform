# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-10-19

U.S. supply chains saw fresh investment across trucking, ports, rail, and manufacturing, while new survey data shows procurement AI adoption remains slow despite costly manual workflows.

## Trucking & Infrastructure

- The U.S. DOT awarded $200 million to 14 truck parking projects across 12 states, giving drivers more places to stop and rest [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/dot-200-million-truck-parking-grants-12-states)

- Pennsylvania is investing $371.5 million into an $837 million Philadelphia port expansion featuring a new berth, added land, and equipment for container and vehicle cargo [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/philadelphia-port-837-million-expansion-new-berth)

## Rail & Equipment Leasing

- Ares is acquiring railcar lessor Infinity from KKR for $2.9 billion, taking over a fleet of 50,000 cars leased to more than 350 customers [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ares-infinity-railcar-leasing-deal)

## Technology & Procurement

- Carrier Logistics and LSP44 are deploying AI to identify shipments with missing tracking numbers, aiming to cut calls and emails to carrier service teams [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/carrier-logistics-lsp44-ai-freight-tracking)

- An Ivalua survey finds only 34% of companies use procurement AI in everyday work, with manual procurement estimated to cost $1.54 million annually per organization as unreliable data slows adoption [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ivalua-procurement-ai-survey-manual-work-costs)

## Manufacturing & Energy

- Hitachi Energy plans a transformer insulation factory worth more than $330 million in London, Ontario, with completion expected by 2028 [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/hitachi-energy-ontario-transformer-insulation-factory)
