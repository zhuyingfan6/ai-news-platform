# Supply Chain 24/7 Daily News Brief - 2026-10-19

U.S. supply chains saw fresh investment in truck parking, port capacity, and transformer manufacturing, while new data highlighted slow AI adoption in procurement and AI-driven gains in freight tracking.

## Infrastructure & Capacity

- The U.S. DOT awarded $200 million for 14 truck parking projects across 12 states to give drivers more places to stop and rest [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/dot-200-million-truck-parking-grants-12-states)

- Philadelphia's port is planning an $837 million expansion, backed by a $371.5 million Pennsylvania investment, adding a new berth, more land, and equipment to handle additional container and vehicle cargo [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/philadelphia-port-837-million-expansion-new-berth)

- Hitachi Energy plans a transformer insulation factory costing more than $330 million in London, Ontario, with completion expected by 2028 [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/hitachi-energy-ontario-transformer-insulation-factory)

## Technology & Digitalization

- Carrier Logistics and LSP44 are using AI to identify shipments with missing tracking numbers and reduce calls and emails to carrier service teams [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/carrier-logistics-lsp44-ai-freight-tracking)

- An Ivalua survey found that just 34% of companies have put procurement AI into everyday use, with manual procurement work estimated to cost $1.54 million annually per organization as unreliable data slows adoption [Read more](https://www.supplychain247.com/article/ivalua-procurement-ai-survey-manual-work-costs)
